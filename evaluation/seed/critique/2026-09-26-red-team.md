# Red-Team Critique of Draft Seed Cases 001–006

- **Date:** 2026-09-26
- **Method:** A separate model context played an engine records clerk and a
  continued-airworthiness engineer. It read AD 2025-19-13, the six draft cases,
  and the reference derivation, and returned questions only. It gave no grades.
- **Limit:** The red team is the same underlying model as the labeler. Its
  findings are prompts to check, not evidence. Each item below was verified by
  the project against the source text or code before any change was made.

## Open questions carried to the reviewer or FAA

- Is reading A practical at all? Returning an engine to service with the hub
  installed and removing it within 60 more FC implies a second shop visit. That
  bears on whether A is a genuine reading. Carried in the FAA email draft.

  asset schema's single `cycles_since_new` may be too thin.
