# Questions for the Recruited Reviewer

One batched list for the qualified reviewer (issue #9), so the reviewer is asked
once. Each item names the cases it would settle. Do not send these piecemeal.

## Interpretation

1. **AD 2026-17-03, paragraph (g): carry forward or lapse?** "At the next
   engine shop visit after the effective date where the 3rd stage HPC rotor
   blade is exposed." If the first qualifying shop visit after the effective
   date does not expose a blade, is replacement required at a later visit where
   one is exposed, or is the requirement spent? *Settles seed-014.*
2. **AD 2025-19-13, paragraph (g): shop visit after the first 100 FC.** If a
   qualifying shop visit occurs after the 100-FC window but before the hub
   reaches its limit, must the hub be removed at that visit, or may it remain
   until the limit? The FAA engineer named in the AD was asked; if no reply
   arrives, ask the reviewer. *Settles seed-001, seed-007, and seed-008
   timing.*
3. **Is P/N 2A5001 the same part in every AD?** AD 2025-19-13 calls P/N
   2A5001 an "HPT 1st-stage hub"; ADs 2021-11-51 and 2022-02-09 call it an
   "HPT 1st-stage disk". Are these the same part, so records should match by
   P/N rather than by name? *Affects multi-directive matching.*
